# Signing up and signing in

On NgatmaDorën, signing up and signing in are the same page. There is no separate registration form: the first time you sign in, your account is created for you.

## Three ways to sign in

**With Google or Facebook.** Press the matching button and confirm at the provider. This is the fastest way.

**With an email link.** This is the main way and it needs no password:

1. Enter your email address and press "Continue with email".
2. Open the email we send you and click the link inside.
3. A confirmation page opens. Press the button there and you are in.

The link works once and then expires. If you click it twice, or try it much later, just ask for a new link from the same page. If the email has not arrived within a few minutes, check your spam folder.

**With a password.** If you prefer a password, press "Use a password instead" on the sign-in page. If you have forgotten it, the "Forgot your password?" link emails you a reset.

## Your first time

After your first sign-in you are asked one thing: whether you will use NgatmaDorën as an individual or as a business. This step cannot be skipped, because it decides what you can do: individuals create campaigns and petitions, while businesses appear as verified backers and post jobs. The rest of the welcome steps are optional and you can fill them in later.

## Confirming your email

Some actions, such as creating a campaign or backing one, need a confirmed email address. We send you a confirmation link; if you lose it, you can request another from the page that appears.

## If you have two-factor authentication on

After the first step you will be asked for the code from your authenticator app. See [Two-factor authentication](/help/account/two-factor-authentication).
