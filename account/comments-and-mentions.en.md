# Comments and mentions

Every campaign has a space underneath for comments and questions. It is meant as a conversation between the initiator and the people who actually backed the campaign, not as an open comment section.

## Who can comment

The campaign initiator, the listed collaborators, our team, and the campaign's backers can all write. A backer here means someone who contributed to the campaign while signed in, or who has a standing pledge on it. A contribution that was refunded no longer counts, and a contribution made as a guest, without an account, does not let you comment. If you are not signed in, you will see an invitation to sign in. If you are signed in but none of the above applies to you, you will see a note explaining that, and you can still read everyone else's comments as normal.

## How to comment

1. Open the campaign page and scroll to "Comments & questions".
2. Write your message in the text box.
3. Press "Post". Your comment appears immediately.

Comments are plain text. Line breaks are kept, but HTML is not allowed: whatever you type is shown exactly as written, never interpreted as code.

## Mentions with @

Under the text box you will find the names you can mention on that campaign: the initiator and their team. Press one and it is inserted where your cursor is, or type it yourself as @username.

A recognised mention turns into a link to that member's profile and the person is notified. A name that is not recognised simply stays as text, with no link and no notification.

## Deleting a comment

You can delete your own comment at any time: the "Delete" button appears underneath it. The campaign initiator can delete any comment on their own campaign, so they can keep the conversation clean.

## If you are asked to wait

There is a limit in place against abuse: several comments one after another within a minute and you will be asked to wait. Give it a minute and try again.
