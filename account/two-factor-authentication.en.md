# Two-factor authentication

Two-factor authentication adds a second step when you sign in: on top of your email or password, you also enter a six-digit code from your authenticator app. It is open to everyone and mandatory only for staff accounts.

## How to turn it on

1. Sign in and choose "Profile" from the account menu at the top right.
2. Go to the "Account" tab and scroll to the "Security" section.
3. Under "Two-factor authentication", press "Enable".
4. Scan the QR code with your authenticator app, such as Google Authenticator, Authy or 1Password. If scanning will not work, type the key in by hand; it is shown under the QR code.
5. Enter the code your app shows and press "Confirm".

## Recovery codes: do not skip this part

As soon as you confirm, you are shown a list of recovery codes. **That is the only time they are shown.** Save them somewhere you can reach without your phone: in your password manager, or printed on paper.

If you lose your phone, a recovery code is your way back in. Each one works once. When you are running low, you can generate a fresh set from the same page.

## What signing in looks like afterwards

After you clear the first step, a page appears asking for your code. Enter the current code from your app, or a recovery code if you do not have your device with you.

If a code is rejected, the reason is almost always the clock: codes change every 30 seconds and your app needs the right time. Wait for the next code and try again.

## How to turn it off

On the same "Account" tab, press "Disable". If your account has a password, you will be asked to enter it to confirm.
