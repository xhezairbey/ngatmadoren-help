# Your panel: an overview

Once you are signed in, everything that belongs to you lives in four places. This is the short map of what sits where.

## Dashboard

This is where the things you started live. For each of your campaigns you see its status, the goal amount and, when the time comes, the payout owed to you. From the same row you can open:

- **View backers**, the report showing who paid what and which rewards you have to fulfil.
- **Manage updates**, where you write the news for your backers.
- **Edit** or **Delete draft**, while the campaign is still a draft.
- **Propose changes**, if the campaign is public and you need a correction.

Below your campaigns sit your petitions, working the same way.

## My backing

This is where the things you supported live: your pledges and your contributions, in one list. If a campaign succeeded and your pledge is waiting to be paid, the "Pay now" button is right here.

## Notifications

Your in-app inbox: updates from campaigns you back, new backing on your own campaigns, mentions, outcomes and reminders. What arrives here and what arrives by email is up to you, on the "Notifications" tab of your profile.

## Profile

Your account settings: name, username, photo, email, language, payout account, security and verification. See [Editing your profile](/help/account/editing-your-profile).

## If something is not showing

Some surfaces depend on your situation. The button to create a campaign does not appear on business accounts, because businesses back campaigns rather than create them. Update management only appears once a campaign is public. If you think something should be there and it is not, write to us.
