# Listed or unlisted

At the last step of creating a campaign, "Review & submit", you choose who can find your campaign: **Listed** or **Unlisted (link only)**.

## Listed

The default. A listed campaign appears on the campaigns page, in its category, in search, on your profile and in search engines such as Google.

## Unlisted (link only)

An unlisted campaign is shown nowhere on the platform: not on the campaigns page, not in a category, not in search, not on the homepage, not on your profile. It is left out of the sitemap, and search engines are told not to index it.

The campaign page itself still works for anyone who has the link. They can read it, back it and share it. Unlisted is not the same as secret: anyone holding the link can pass it on to someone else.

## While the campaign is a draft

You can switch between "Listed" and "Unlisted (link only)" on the review step as many times as you like, right up until you submit the campaign for review.

## After the campaign goes live

Visibility can then move only one way. An **unlisted campaign can be made listed**, but a **listed campaign can never be made unlisted**. Once a campaign has appeared on the campaigns page, been indexed by a search engine or been shared and embedded, none of that can be pulled back, so the platform does not offer to.

If your campaign is unlisted, your dashboard shows an "Unlisted" marker next to it and a "Make listed" button. Confirm once and the campaign starts appearing everywhere a listed campaign does. The link you already shared keeps working exactly as it did.

A campaign that staff closed with a refund after an investigation cannot be made listed.

## Finding the link to an unlisted campaign

Since nothing on the platform points at it, the link lives on your dashboard: press "View campaign" next to it, then share that address the way you would share any campaign. See [sharing your campaign](/help/campaigns/sharing-your-campaign).

## Everything else stays the same

Visibility changes only where a campaign is shown. Review, verification, the payout hold and the refund rules work exactly as they do for a listed campaign; an unlisted campaign gets no lighter review.
