# Editing a live campaign

Before your campaign passes review you can edit it freely. Once it is live, the text is locked and changes go through staff.

## Why it locks

People pledge based on what they read. If the story, the budget or the rewards could change after money has been pledged, the pledge would mean nothing. The lock protects your backers, and with them, you.

## Proposing changes

1. Open your campaign and choose "Propose changes".
2. Make the edits you want.
3. Press "Submit for review".

Staff read them and approve or not. Until it is decided you have one request under review and cannot send another. If you change your mind, withdraw the request and start a new one.

If you have not changed anything, there is nothing to submit.

## What never changes

- **The funding goal.** Nor the minimum.
- **The deadline.**
- **The funding model.**
- **A reward tier's price and limit.** The tier's wording can be fixed, the figures cannot.

These are the terms on which people decided to give money. If they genuinely no longer work, write to us from the contact form and we will look at the case with you.

## What you can change without asking

You can post [updates](/help/campaigns/posting-updates) at any time. An update is where you explain what changed without touching the original promise. In most cases, that is the right answer.
