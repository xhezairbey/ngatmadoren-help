# Posting updates

An update is how you tell your backers where the work has got to. Silence is the number one reason people end up disappointed by a campaign, even when everything is going fine.

## How to post an update

1. Sign in and open your dashboard.
2. Find your campaign and press "Manage updates".
3. Write a short title and the body. Markdown is supported, so you can use headings, lists and links.
4. Press "Publish update".

The update appears on the public campaign page straight away and your backers are notified.

## When you can post

Updates can only be published once the campaign is public. While it is still a draft or under review the option does not appear: there is nobody there to read it yet.

## If you get something wrong

That is fine. From the same page you can edit or delete an update of yours. Do note that the notification sent when you published it cannot be taken back, so one quick read-through before publishing is worth it.

## What to write

- What has concretely happened since last time, even if it is small.
- What comes next and when you expect it.
- If something has slipped, say so openly and give a new date. Backers understand a delay that is explained; they do not understand silence.

Petitions have updates too, and they work the same way: you post them from your petition page, and signers read them.
