# Crediting your team

Few campaigns are one person's work. You can credit the people working with you, so the campaign page shows who is really behind it.

## How it works

You invite a member and propose a role. They receive an invitation and decide for themselves.

- If they accept, their name and profile link appear on the campaign team.
- If they decline, or simply do nothing, nothing is shown.

Nobody appears on your campaign without accepting. This is deliberate: having your name on a campaign that asks for money is something a person should choose.

## Inviting someone

1. Open your campaign from your panel.
2. Find the team section.
3. Invite the member and write the proposed role.

If someone declines and later changes their mind, you can invite them again.

## What a collaborator can do

Nothing. The credit is display only.

Collaborators cannot edit the campaign, post updates, see backers or touch the money. The campaign stays yours, and so does the responsibility.

If you want someone else to actually run a campaign, that campaign should be theirs.

## When to use it

- To credit co-founders, mentors or a partner organisation.
- To give a new campaign weight by showing people the community already knows.

Only invite people who are genuinely involved. A team padded with names is noticeable, and reviewers notice it too.
