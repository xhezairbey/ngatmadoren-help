# Seeing who backed you

Once your campaign is public, you can see who has backed it, how much, and what you are owed. It is all on your campaign's backers page.

## Where it is

Open your dashboard and choose "View backers" next to the campaign.

You will find three things:

**The payout summary.** How much was collected, what is deducted, how much you are owed and how much has already been paid. On a keep-what-you-raise campaign the deduction is the processing costs; on an all-or-nothing campaign it is the platform fee. See [fees and payouts](/help/campaigns/fees-and-payouts).

**Paid pledges.** Only for all-or-nothing campaigns: who paid, how much, and which reward they are entitled to, with the estimated delivery date. This is the list you fulfil rewards from. On a keep-what-you-raise campaign it stays empty, with the note "No paid pledges yet."

**Direct contributions.** Who contributed and how much. A contribution carries no reward to fulfil. A backer who gave as a guest is shown by their email address.

## Downloading the list

Press "Download CSV". The file carries the name and amount of everyone who paid, plus the reward and delivery for a pledge, so you can work outside the platform, for example to thank your backers or send rewards.

## Anonymous backers

On an all-or-nothing campaign, some backers pledge without appearing publicly. You still see them in this list because you need to know where to send a reward, but they are marked as publicly anonymous. Do not name them on the campaign, in updates or on social media.

## What to do with this data

This is personal data belonging to people who trusted you. Use it only for this campaign: to thank your backers and to deliver anything you promised. Do not add it to a mailing list and do not pass it to anyone else.

If someone asks you to delete their details, write to us from the contact form.
