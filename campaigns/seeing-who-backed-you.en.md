# Seeing who backed you

After collection you need to know who paid, how much, and what they are owed. It is all on your campaign's backers page.

## Where it is

Open your campaign from your panel and choose the backers page.

You will find three things:

**The payout summary.** How much was collected, how much the platform keeps, how much you are owed and how much has already been paid.

**Paid pledges.** Who paid, how much, and which reward they are entitled to, with the estimated delivery date. This is the list you fulfil rewards from.

**Direct contributions.** Money collected with no reward to fulfil.

## Downloading the list

Press "Download CSV". The file carries the name, amount, reward and delivery, so you can work outside the platform while preparing postage.

## Anonymous backers

Some backers choose not to appear publicly. You still see them in this list because you need to know where to send a reward, but they are marked as publicly anonymous. Do not name them on the campaign, in updates or on social media.

## What to do with this data

This is personal data belonging to people who trusted you. Use it only to deliver what you promised. Do not add it to a mailing list and do not pass it to anyone else.

If someone asks you to delete their details, write to us from the contact form.
