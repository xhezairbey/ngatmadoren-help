# Sharing your campaign

Campaigns are not found by accident. Most of the money comes from people someone invited directly.

## Copying the link

On the campaign page, find the share section and press "Copy link". This is the link you send in chat, by email or on social media.

The link works for anyone, with or without an account. People can contribute as guests. See [contributing as a guest](/help/backing/contributing-as-a-guest).

If your campaign is [unlisted](/help/campaigns/listed-or-unlisted), this link is the only way anyone finds it: the platform shows it nowhere else. Your dashboard is where you get it, next to the campaign's "View campaign" link.

## Putting the campaign on another site

If you or your organisation have a website, you can embed the campaign there. Press "Copy embed code" and paste that code into your page.

What appears is a small box with the title, the meter and a button through to the campaign. It updates itself, so you never have to touch it again.

This is useful for clubs, schools and businesses that want to keep the campaign in view without sending people away.

## What actually works

- **Ask people directly.** One personal message weighs more than ten public posts.
- **Start with the close circle.** A campaign that begins empty is hard to back. Family and friends stop that feeling.
- **Post [updates](/help/campaigns/posting-updates).** Every update is a fresh reason for someone to share the campaign.
- **Show where the money is going.** People give to a plan, not to a number.

## If you want help

Ambassadors help campaigns in their area. See [becoming an ambassador](/help/backing/becoming-an-ambassador) to understand the role, or write to us if you would like to be put in touch with one.
