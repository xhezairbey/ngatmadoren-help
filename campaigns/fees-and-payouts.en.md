# Fees and payouts

What the platform keeps depends on the funding model you chose when you created the campaign. There are no hidden fees, and it costs nothing to start a campaign.

## All-or-nothing campaigns

The platform keeps 5% of the funds collected. That is our revenue and it is not waived for anyone. Importantly, it is calculated on what was actually collected, not on what was pledged, and it is only taken if the campaign succeeded. If the campaign falls short of its minimum, nothing is collected and nothing is kept.

## Keep-what-you-raise campaigns

The platform keeps no percentage at all. The only deduction is a flat, at-cost card-processing rate, currently 1.5% (an estimate until a payment provider is chosen). It covers the real cost of accepting cards, which varies by the card's issuing country, and we keep the rate in line with what we are actually charged, with no margin added. Your payout breakdown shows this deduction on your own figures. See [fees](/fees) for the full picture, including why the rate varies by country.

## Before you can be paid: your payout account

Funds cannot reach you without a connected bank account. Connect it once and you are done:

1. Sign in and choose "Profile" from the account menu at the top right.
2. Go to the "Account" tab.
3. Under "Payout account", enter your IBAN.
4. Save. You will see a marker showing the account is connected.

## How and when you get paid

On an all-or-nothing campaign, the collection window opens after the deadline: around 14 days in which backers pay their pledges. If you need more time you can extend that window once, from your dashboard. When the window closes, the amount owed to you is recorded and the payout is prepared.

On a keep-what-you-raise campaign there is no collection window: the money arrives while the campaign is still open, and the amount owed to you is recorded when the campaign closes.

In both cases you can follow the figures on your dashboard, and the "View backers" report tells you who paid what and which rewards you have to fulfil.

## Money does not flow backwards

There are no refunds on the platform, and that is structure rather than policy: on an all-or-nothing campaign no card is charged before the outcome is known, and on a keep-what-you-raise campaign the backer knows from the start that their contribution stays with the cause.
