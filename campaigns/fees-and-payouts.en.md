# Fees and payouts

Every new campaign is keep-what-you-raise. This page covers what is deducted from what you raise, when the money reaches you, and the one case in which money goes back to a backer. There are no hidden fees, and it costs nothing to start a campaign.

## What is deducted

The platform keeps no percentage at all. The only deduction is a flat, at-cost card-processing rate, currently 1.5% (an estimate until a payment provider is chosen). It covers the real cost of accepting cards, which varies by the card's issuing country, and we keep the rate in line with what we are actually charged, with no margin added. Your payout breakdown shows this deduction on your own figures. See [fees](/fees) for the full picture, including why the rate varies by country.

## If your campaign uses all-or-nothing

All-or-nothing is not offered for new campaigns at the moment. On a campaign that already uses it, the platform keeps 5% of the funds collected. That is our revenue and it is not waived for anyone. It is calculated on what was actually collected, not on what was pledged, and it is only taken if the campaign succeeded. If the campaign falls short of its minimum, nothing is collected and nothing is kept.

## Before you can be paid: your payout account

Funds cannot reach you without a connected bank account. Connect it once and you are done:

1. Sign in and choose "Profile" from the account menu at the top right.
2. Go to the "Account" tab.
3. Under "Payout account", enter your IBAN.
4. Save. You will see a marker showing the account is connected.

## How and when you get paid

Contributions arrive while the campaign is still open. At the deadline, the amount owed to you is recorded. The payout is then held for a set period before it is paid to you, once your payout account is verified. [How it works](/how-it-works) says how long the hold lasts.

On an all-or-nothing campaign, the collection window opens after the deadline instead: around 14 days in which backers pay their pledges. If you need more time you can [extend that window](/help/campaigns/extending-the-collection-window) once, from your dashboard. When the window closes, the amount owed to you is recorded, and the same hold runs from that moment.

In both cases you can follow the figures on your dashboard, and the "View backers" report tells you [who backed you](/help/campaigns/seeing-who-backed-you) and how much.

## When money goes back

Money flows from backers, to the platform, to you. There is one exception, and only staff can make it.

Until your payout is made, staff can put your campaign under investigation, for example after a report. A campaign under investigation takes no new contributions, and its payout is frozen until the investigation ends. A report alone never pauses a campaign; a staff member decides that. The hold exists to give that decision time to happen.

If staff find a real problem, the affected contributions are refunded in full, including any tip the backer added. If the campaign itself is the problem, every contribution is refunded and the campaign ends without a payout.

That is the only way money moves backwards. There is no refund on request, backers cannot refund themselves, there are no partial refunds, and once you have been paid, there is no refund at all.
