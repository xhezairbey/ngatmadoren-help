# Extending the collection window

When an all-or-nothing campaign reaches its minimum, the collection window opens: the time during which backers are asked to complete their payment. If some pledges are still unpaid as it ends, you can extend it once.

## When it helps

An extension helps when you have a number of unpaid pledges from people who look like they will pay and simply have not got to it. Holidays, travel and card problems are all common.

It does not help when pledges have been abandoned. An extension creates no new money and brings no new backers. The campaign does not accept new pledges during collection.

## How to do it

1. Open your campaign from your panel.
2. Choose "Extend the collection window".

You can do this once only. Once used, there is no second time, so do not spend it on the first day.

## What else to do

An extension only adds time. People pay when they are reminded and when they trust you.

- Post an [update](/help/campaigns/posting-updates) thanking those who have paid and reminding the rest.
- Keep the same tone you had during the campaign. This is not an invoice, it is a reminder.

The platform sends automatic reminders before the window closes. Those are essential emails and backers cannot switch them off.

## When it closes

Unpaid pledges are not collected. You receive what was collected, less the platform's share. See [fees and payouts](/help/campaigns/fees-and-payouts).
