# Signing a petition

A petition is the community's voice, not its money. There are no payments, no pledges and no payment deadlines. You are simply adding your name to a call for change.

## The steps

1. Open the petition page and read what it asks for and who it is addressed to.
2. Sign in. Signatures have to belong to a real person, so an account is required; business accounts cannot sign petitions.
3. If you want to, add a short message explaining why the issue matters to you. It is optional and appears next to your signature.
4. If you would rather your name did not appear publicly, tick "Sign anonymously".
5. Press "Sign". Your signature is counted straight away.

## About signing anonymously

Anonymous means your name is not shown in the public list of signers. The petition starter and our team can still see who you are, because a signature with no trace behind it would carry no weight with decision makers.

## What happens next

The petition starter can post updates as the cause moves forward. If the petition wins, you get told. You can adjust what you are notified about under Settings, on the "Notifications" tab.

You can sign a petition only once. If you see a message saying the petition is not accepting signatures, it means the petition has closed, has been delivered to its decision maker, or has been marked a victory.
