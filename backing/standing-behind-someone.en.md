# Standing behind someone

On NgatmaDorën you can publicly stand behind another member. It is the community's way of saying "I know this person and I vouch for them".

## Why it matters

A profile with people behind it reads differently from an empty one. When someone opens a campaign and sees that five members stand behind the creator, they have something concrete to go on.

This matters most for people who have just arrived on the platform and do not yet have a finished campaign behind them.

## How to do it

1. Open the person's profile.
2. Press "Stand behind".
3. If you like, add a short note about how you know them. This step is optional, but a note is worth far more than a number.
4. You can also say how you know them, or leave that unsaid.

You need to be signed in.

## Withdrawing

You can withdraw at any time from the same profile. The person is not notified when you withdraw.

## What it is not

This is not an identity check and it does not make anyone verified by the platform. Verification is a separate process, carried out by staff. See [verification](/help/campaigns/verification).

Stand behind people you actually know. The value of the whole thing depends on it.
