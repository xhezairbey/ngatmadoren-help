# Choosing a reward

Some all-or-nothing campaigns offer rewards: a concrete thank-you for the amount you pledge. Rewards are optional. Plenty of good campaigns have none at all, and a pledge without a reward counts just as much.

## How they work

On the campaign page, below the amount, you will see the reward tiers. Each tier has a price and, sometimes, a limited number of places. When you type your amount, you are shown which tier it reaches.

If you pledge more than a tier's price, that tier is yours. You do not have to find the exact figure.

## What to know

- Rewards exist only on all-or-nothing campaigns. Keep-what-you-raise campaigns collect direct contributions with no rewards. See [the two funding models](/help/campaigns/choosing-a-funding-model).
- A reward is not an order. You are backing a project, and the reward is the creator's thank-you.
- The delivery date you see is the creator's estimate, not a promise from the platform.
- If the campaign does not reach its minimum, nobody is charged and no reward comes into being.

## Who delivers the reward

The creator, not us. After collection, the creator sees the list of backers who paid and which reward each is entitled to, and contacts you directly.

If something goes wrong with delivery, start by writing to the creator on the campaign page. If you get no answer, write to us from the contact form.
