# Becoming an ambassador

Ambassadors are members who raise their hand first in their city, neighbourhood or campus. The programme exists because every good idea in our community dies in the same place: nobody goes first.

## What an ambassador does

- Knows the clubs, the initiatives and the people who need a hand, and brings them to the platform before the idea fades.
- Helps a few campaigns through a semester: from the guide and the fee calculator through to launch, so their first campaign is a good one rather than an improvised one.
- Stands as the first point of contact for people in their area who do not know where to start.

You are not promoting a platform. You are giving your community direction.

## How to apply

1. Open the [Become an Ambassador](/ambassadors) page.
2. Read the role and the perks.
3. Press the apply button and fill in the form.

You need an account.

## After you apply

We read applications in turn. Being accepted is not automatic: the role carries responsibility toward the people who will rely on you, so the decision is made by hand.

If you do not hear back immediately, that is not a no. The programme grows slowly and carefully.

## If you are not an ambassador

You do not need the role to help. You can back campaigns, [stand behind people you know](/help/backing/standing-behind-someone), and share campaigns with your circle. Ambassador is a named role, not permission to be useful.
