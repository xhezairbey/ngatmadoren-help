# Reporting something to staff

If you come across a campaign, a comment or a profile that should not be there, report it. Staff read every report.

## How to report

1. Open the thing you want to report: the campaign, the comment or the profile.
2. Find the "Report" control on that page.
3. Choose a reason from the list.
4. Write what staff should know. The more concrete you are, the faster we can act.
5. Send it.

You need to be signed in to report. That helps us tell real reports apart from abuse of the form itself.

## What happens next

Nothing happens automatically. A person looks first, always. No campaign is removed and no account is closed on the strength of a single report.

Your report goes to staff only. The person you report is not told who sent it.

If you have already reported the same thing, we will tell you the report has been received. There is no need to send it twice.

## What is worth reporting

Content that misleads, hate speech, harassment, or a campaign that does not look genuine. If you are in doubt, report it. Better to bring it to our attention than to leave it.

For a disagreement with an initiator about a reward, start on the campaign page or with the contact form. Reporting is for content that breaks the rules, not for customer service.
