# Becoming a reviewer

Reviewers are volunteer members who read campaigns and score them before staff decide. They are the trust layer behind every campaign you see live.

## What a reviewer does

The role is light and advisory. Your score guides the staff decision, it does not replace it.

- Read campaigns awaiting review and score them on four criteria: clarity, feasibility, impact and budget.
- Leave a short reasoning, and recommend: approve, request changes, or reject.
- Serve with recorded consent: the reviewer agreement.

## How to apply

1. Open the [Become a reviewer](/reviewers) page.
2. Press the apply button.
3. Write why you would make a good reviewer, with any relevant experience.

You need an account.

## Applying is not being granted

The founders read every application and grant the role by hand. Reviewing is a responsibility, never something a pledge or a raised total can buy.

## The reviewer agreement

Before you start, you are asked to accept the reviewer agreement. It explains what is expected of you and how impartiality is kept. Your consent is recorded against the version you accepted, so what you agreed to is always known.

If the text changes later, you will be asked to accept it again.
